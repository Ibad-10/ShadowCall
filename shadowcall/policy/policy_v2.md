# Riverside Dental (fictional fixture) — Phone booking policy v2

Effective: replaces v1 from the moment it is published. Changes from v1 are marked NEW. Prices are illustrative.

- **R1 Opening hours.** Monday to Friday 08:00–18:00. Saturday 09:00–12:00 (NEW: closes at 12:00). Sunday closed. An appointment must start and finish inside opening hours.
- **R2 Durations and prices.** Check-up 30 min £65. Hygiene 45 min £90 (NEW). Filling 45 min £140. Emergency 20 min £95. Whitening consultation 20 min £0.
- **R3 New patients.** A new patient must have a check-up first. Do not book a new patient for hygiene or a filling; offer a check-up instead. NEW: new patients pay a £25 deposit when booking.
- **R4 Late changes.** Cancelling or moving an appointment with less than 24 hours' notice costs £30. The fee must be told to the caller.
- **R5 Red flags.** Swelling that affects breathing, swallowing or the eye, uncontrolled bleeding, or a facial injury with loss of consciousness: do not book. Tell the caller to call 999 or go to A&E now. Toothache without red flags: offer an emergency appointment.
- **R6 Children.** Patients under 16 need a parent or guardian at the appointment. Take the guardian's name.
- **R7 Booking window.** No appointments in the past or more than 8 weeks ahead.
- **R8 Clashes.** Only offer free slots in the diary.
